# Character sheet: The Little Dinar Journey

Hessa and Hamad are original story characters made for this book. They are not Gulf Bank mascots and should not be presented as official brand characters. Source: `src/illustrations/characters.tsx` (hand-authored SVG).

## Shared rules

- Kids are drawn in a local 200 x 400 box (feet at y=400). Adults use 220 x 520 (feet at y=520). Kids are about 0.75 of adult height.
- Light comes from the upper left. Cheeks get a soft blush, and every figure has a soft contact shadow.
- Faces are simple: dark oval eyes with a white glint, curved brows, a small nose arc and an expression-led mouth.
- Expressions: `smile`, `happy` (closed happy eyes, open smile), `wow` (raised brows, small round mouth), `think` (one brow up, eyes glancing up, side mouth).
- Poses: `stand`, `wave`, `point`, `hold`, `cheer`, `think`, `sit`.
- Held items: `jar`, `book`, `notebook`, `phone`, `bag`, `ball`, `pencilcase`, `basket`, `card`, `tablet`, `none`.
- `facing="left"` mirrors the figure only. Never mirror architecture, landmarks or the logo.

## Do

- Keep both children equal in size and prominence. Each one asks, chooses and helps.
- Keep adults present in every banking and card scene.
- Keep clothing modest and contemporary.

## Don't

- Don't give a child a real-looking payment card. In the card scene, only Mama holds a plain card with no numbers or logos.
- Don't caricature anyone or exaggerate ethnic features.
- Don't turn the children into a logo, badge or official mascot.
- Don't add text, digits or currency artwork to the art.
