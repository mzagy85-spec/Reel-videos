LEARNING BY DOING

Every page has something to try

All amounts are fictional learning examples and are labelled that way. Money is calculated in whole fils, so the arithmetic is always exact.

Page 3. Ten 100-fils learning tokens make one dinar (1.000 KWD).

Page 7. The basket is 100 fils over budget, so paying is blocked until the stickers go back.

Page 10. Deposits and withdrawals move money between wallet and account. The total stays 5.000 KWD.

Page 12. A practice message asks for a code. The safe answer: stop and tell a grown-up.

TWO GAMES

A neighbourhood map puzzle and a bilingual crossword

Page 9, Money Journey Map. Children assemble a Kuwaiti neighbourhood (tap, keyboard or drag), then match money missions to places: plan at home, compare prices at the co-op, deposit at the bank.

Page 14, Money Words crossword. A separate grid for each language, read right to left in Arabic. Children pick words from a word bank or type them; a clue list gives screen-reader users the same game. An optional four-question quiz follows.

SAFE FOR CHILDREN AND FOR THE BANK

Nothing a child does here touches real banking

No personal data
The book never asks for a name, photo, age, email, phone or account number. Progress is saved on the device only if a parent turns it on.
No banking connection
No login, no banking APIs, no real transactions, no payment screens and no PIN entry. Every simulation is labelled as one.
No tracking
No analytics, ads or third-party scripts. The book loads nothing from outside the bank's own servers.
Correct money facts
1 KWD = 1,000 fils, real coin values, no other currencies. Prices are marked as learning examples.
Calm safety lesson
Children learn to stop, close a strange message and ask a parent, without fear and without sharing any code.
No pressure
No timers, lives, leaderboards or purchases. A keepsake card at the end, not a certificate.
